I am an Airman in the US Air Force with a growing interest in the intersection of linguistics, aeronautics, space exploration, and space research.  I earned a PhD in Linguistics from New York University. My dissertation argues for a unified theory for deriving _wh_-questions and relative clauses, drawing on data from three dialects of Ewe. My linguistic research focuses on syntactic theory, micro-comparative syntax, predicative and nominal possession, argument structure, A-bar phenomena, and dialectology.

As a musician by avocation, I serve as an occasional accompanist (keyboard) in the Evangelical Presbyterian Church. I enjoy reading Aristotle, Descartes, Dostoevsky, Epictetus, Hitchens, Marcus Aurelius, Montaigne, Nkrumah, Nietzsche, Plato,  Russell, and Seneca. I am an FC Barcelona fan, and I love (Ghana😉)jollof rice!
